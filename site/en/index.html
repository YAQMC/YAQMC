01 / PLAYBACK

A rhythm of its own

A Rust playback service keeps your queue, progress and quality in sync. Sources depend on account rights and platform availability, without bypassing membership or copyright restrictions.

Explore audio quality ↗
02 / LYRICS

Words, wherever you listen

Word-by-word lyrics, translations and romanization follow the music. Desktop builds also offer lyric overlays and Lyrics Island; use the system tray to unlock them.

Explore lyrics ↗
03 / LIBRARY

Follow a favorite further

Search songs, artists and albums, then follow the connections between them. Sign in to access your account library, and use share links to pick up your next listen.

Installation guide

Signed APK with phone and tablet layouts. No desktop lyric overlays, plugins or Local API in this version.

Check the SHA-256 files in the Release before installing. Checksums detect corruption; they do not independently verify the publisher. Desktop auto-update feeds are currently x64 only.

Make room for your ideas

A player with
your own touches.

Extend the desktop app with styles, lyric scenes or platform components. Download a .yaqmc-plugin file, then install and review its permissions in Settings → Plugins.

Examples are disabled by default and are unverified local sideloads, not a marketplace. Platform examples use synthetic data, not a real music service.
