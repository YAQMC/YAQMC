Yet Another Q Music Client

Music, lyrics, and desktop—
on one clock.

YAQMC is an unofficial QQ Music client for Windows and Linux. Native audio, word-timed lyrics, account library, and desktop integrations project one authoritative player state.

Download beta View source

Not affiliated with Tencent or QQ Music. No subscription, regional, copyright, or entitlement bypass.

One state, many surfaces

A desktop player should agree with itself.

01

Native playback, not a guessed browser clock

Rust owns queue, seek, shuffle/repeat, preview timelines, quality, and errors. Media sessions and lyrics follow the real audio engine.

02

Word timing and lyric overlays

Immersive Lyrics, Desktop Lyrics, and Lyrics Island support translation, romanization, and a dedicated unlock control.

03

Restricted QQ / WeChat OAuth

Sign-in stays on Tencent-hosted pages. Durable credentials live in the OS store; OAuth and lyric WebViews have no account-command access.

04

Quality follows real entitlement

Standard through Master intersect account rights and live source availability. Authorized mflac is decrypted in memory; disk cache stays encrypted.

05

Windows and Linux

Multi-architecture packages, tray, SMTC/MPRIS, output devices, and Linux diagnostics—without hiding native Wayland limits.
